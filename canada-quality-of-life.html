What Spain answers

Everything Mexico almost was.

The instinct to leave is sound, and Mexico is the obvious first look — close, warm, affordable. The reservations are usually the same ones: healthcare you can rely on, safety you don't think about, and a residency status that doesn't expire on a technicality.

Spain answers all three. A top-ranked EU health system, the everyday safety of a Western European country, and the Non-Lucrative Visa — a clear, legal path to living here. Your equity from a Toronto or Vancouver sale goes remarkably far on the Valencia coast, and the Canada–Spain treaty keeps your income from being taxed twice.

It's the warm, affordable, better life you were looking south for — without the compromises that gave you pause.
