A note on tax

Beckham Law has a window.

Spain's special expatriate regime — a flat 24% on Spanish-source income up to a generous ceiling — can be elected only inside a six-month window after you register with Spanish social security. Miss it and it is gone; there is no second application. For a Full AfterLife engagement, the question of whether it fits your situation is on the table from the first call, not discovered at month four when the door is already closing.

It is available as a €2,500-per-person add-on, and it is included outright in the Private Client engagement.
