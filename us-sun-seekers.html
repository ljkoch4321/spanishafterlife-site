ClimateThree hundred days of sun, mild winters, sea swimming from April to November. The pleasant version of warm.

HealthcareA top-ranked public system, with private cover for a couple roughly €240–€470 a month.
What Spain answers

The same idea, done properly.

Moving somewhere warm is the right instinct. The Sun Belt is just the nearest version of it — and increasingly an expensive one, in places that were built for the car rather than for a long, good life.

Spain delivers the climate you were chasing and adds the things Florida and Arizona can't: a thousand-year-old town to walk through, a food culture worth staying in for, and forty countries within easy reach. Frequently at a lower all-in cost than the Sun Belt move you were already prepared to make.

If you were going to do this anyway, it's worth doing once, in the place that gives you the most life per dollar.
