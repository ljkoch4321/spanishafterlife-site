What this is

Not a better version of the service. A different one.

Every Spanish AfterLife engagement is founder-led. The Private Client takes that further.

This is for clients whose relocation complexity, transaction scale, or personal preference calls for something closer to a trusted advisor embedded in the process than a coordinated service they have engaged. The founder is physically present at the key moments. The lifestyle infrastructure is built alongside the legal and property work. The relationship continues for two years after arrival.

Immigration and property outcomes are the same as every other engagement. The Private Client delivers those outcomes and everything around them — the orientation before the search, the weeks before and after completion, the social and lifestyle infrastructure that turns a good relocation into an exceptional one.

The life, not just the address

Lifestyle infrastructure

The AfterLife is not an address. It is a life — and that life has infrastructure that takes years to build on your own. For Private Client engagements, we build it with you from arrival.

How is The Private Client different from The Full AfterLife?
The Full AfterLife is a complete, professionally managed engagement — immigration, property search, legal coordination, post-arrival support. It is excellent. It is not a lite version of Private Client. The Private Client is a different mode of engagement for clients who want the founder directly involved and physically present at the key moments. It adds the Spain Orientation, white-glove property pre-scouting before you fly, post-arrival settlement in person for the first days in your property, lifestyle infrastructure setup, Beckham Law included for all adults, and two years of annual advisory. It also operates under capped availability — the founder's direct time is finite. If The Full AfterLife fits how you want to work, it is the right choice. Private Client is for clients who want something closer to a trusted advisor embedded in the process.
What does "the founder is there" actually mean, practically?
It means the Spain Orientation is three to four days in the Valencia Community together — on the ground, in the areas, at the restaurants, meeting the people. It means every property on the shortlist has been physically walked by the founder before you arrive for viewings. It means the founder attends your viewings, your arras signing, your notary appointment, and is present in Spain for your first few days in the property. It means calls are answered same-day. It means every coordination point — legal, immigration, lifestyle — is handled directly rather than by a team member following a process.
Is the Spain Orientation trip included in the price?
The orientation is included in the sense that the founder's time, curation, introductions, and all hospitality during the trip are part of the Private Client engagement. The client covers their own travel and accommodation. We advise on where to stay based on the areas we're covering together and what we're trying to understand about your preferences. Most clients find the orientation pays for itself immediately in time saved on the property search — you arrive knowing what you're looking for, which changes everything about how fast the search moves.
Why is Beckham Law included rather than an add-on?
Because for Private Client clients, the conversation about income structuring during the Beckham election period is integral to the engagement from day one — not an add-on discovered at month four when the six-month window is already partially closed. At the income levels and transaction scales typical of Private Client, the Beckham decision needs to be designed alongside the broader arrival plan. Including it removes the decision and ensures it's handled correctly from the start.
What if I don't want to buy property — just immigration?
Private Client immigration only — without property — is €14,500 for a couple. It delivers all the same immigration service as The Arrival — Two, plus the Spain Orientation, Beckham Law included, two years of annual advisory, and priority direct access. Some clients use Private Client for immigration and then return for the property search 12–18 months later when they are ready. The relationship continues.
How many Private Client engagements do you take?
We do not publish the number. The honest answer is: not many. When capacity is full, we say so and offer The Full AfterLife or a waiting list. If you have a specific timeline in mind, enquire early. The eligibility call is free and takes 60 minutes — it is always the right starting point.
